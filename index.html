Research

I'm interested in understanding how neural networks learn — how they extract meaningful representations from data, and why their learning dynamics unfold the way they do. I approach this through mechanistic interpretability and deep learning theory, studying the internal structure that emerges during training and what it reveals about generalisation. More broadly, I'm drawn to problems at the interface of mathematics, machine learning, and the brain.
