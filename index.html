I'm a pre-final year student at BITS Pilani (Goa), doing a dual major in Mathematics and Computer Science. I'm very fortunate to pursue a year of my undergraduate thesis under Dr. Alan Yuille (Bloomberg Distinguished Professor of Computer Science and Cognitive Science) at Johns Hopkins University. I hail from Ambernath, however I grew up moving across India.

My current interests centre around understanding Intelligence (Artificial and Biological) and learning mechanics. As Popper said, we are students of problems rather than subject matter, I like to frequently dabble in theoretical neuroscience, statistical physics and pure mathematics. Earlier, I built a Mars rover and spent considerable time on computer vision.

I am currently obsessed with understanding learning dynamics and looking at domain-adaptive continual learning through a lens of mechanistic interpretability for my undergraduate thesis.
