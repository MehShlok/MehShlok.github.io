Hobbies

I grew up across nearly ten different schools all over India, which probably explains why I'm comfortable being thrown into unfamiliar situations and love meeting new people, going on hikes/long trips.

Sports have always been a big part of my life. In an earlier life, I played badminton at the state level. I've also played chess at the district level and still play casually! Horse riding is something I picked up along the way and has stuck around. Other than that I swim, play football and just long walks in the nature are appealing as well!

When I'm not doing any of that, I'm usually reading (I started when I was 4.. with chacha chaudhary, enid blyton, tinkle! transitioning to..) non-fiction, philosophy of mind (Some amazing books such as Being You, give an insight into the workings behind abstract topics as consciouness), that kind of thing. I listen to a lot of music, play guitar occasionally, and I'm almost always in the middle of a sudoku. There's something about a problem with a deterministic answer that I find very calming (Hence Mathematics!).
